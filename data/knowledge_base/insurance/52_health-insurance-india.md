## Why it comes before investing

Medical inflation in India runs well above general inflation, and a single hospitalisation can
wipe out years of savings. This is why health cover is usually treated as the first financial
product a household should own, ahead of any investment. Without it, the money earmarked for
long-term goals becomes the de facto emergency fund.

## Individual, family floater, and top-up

An **individual** policy covers one person for a fixed sum insured. A **family floater** covers
several members from one shared pool — cheaper per head, but if one member has a large claim,
the remaining cover for everyone else shrinks for that year. A **top-up** or super top-up sits
above a deductible: it pays only after claims cross a threshold, which makes a large total cover
affordable. A common structure is a ₹10 lakh floater with a ₹25 lakh super top-up.

## The clauses that decide whether a claim pays

- **Waiting periods.** Typically 30 days for any illness, two to four years for specified
  conditions such as hernia or cataract, and up to three to four years for pre-existing diseases.
- **Room-rent capping.** If the policy caps the room at 1% of sum insured per day and you take a
  costlier room, the insurer can proportionately reduce the *entire* bill, not just the room
  charge.
- **Co-payment.** A fixed share of every claim you must bear, common in senior-citizen plans.
- **Sub-limits.** Per-procedure caps on things like cataract surgery or maternity.
- **Pre- and post-hospitalisation.** Usually 30 and 60 days of related expenses.

Declaring pre-existing conditions honestly at purchase matters more than any discount: a
non-disclosure found at claim time is grounds for rejection, even years later.

## Cashless versus reimbursement

In a network hospital the insurer settles directly. Outside the network you pay and claim back,
which needs every bill, discharge summary, and diagnostic report. Checking that a hospital you
would realistically use is in the network is more practical than counting total network size.
